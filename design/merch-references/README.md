## The pattern across all five

- **Single-color ink** — 4 of 5 use one ink color. Only #2 is two-color.
- **Neutral earthy garments** — cream, white, brown. No black anywhere.
- **Large placement** — 4 of 5 are full back or full front. Only #3 is a small chest hit.
- **Type-led** — 3 of 5 have no illustration at all; the typographic lockup *is* the design.
- **Retro line-art mascot** in 2 of 5 — 1930s rubber-hose style, single color, no shading.
- **Club / heritage copy** — "Coffee Club" ×2, plus "Espresso Lab, New York", "Always
  Caffeinated", "SINCE 2023". Membership and provenance signaling.

Single-color ink on a calm neutral ground is also the netela principle from `DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md`
§1 — quiet field, identity concentrated into one placed element. It is simultaneously the
cheapest thing to screen print. Aesthetics and unit cost agree here; exploit that.

## Constraints that apply

1. **Light garments are blocked.** Both logo files are cream artwork on transparency
   (`DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md` §7). The dark-ink variant is open item #1 in §11. Until it exists,
   only dark garments with cream ink are producible — which is exactly what reference #4 is.
2. **Tricolor as thin lines only** (§3, §6b). Green/gold/oxblood as a ≤4px tilet rule. Never
   as blocks, never as a fill.
3. **No unproofed Amharic** (§4). Do not use ግዕዝ as decoration until someone has read it.
4. **Type is Playfair Display (display) + Inter (body)** (§4). The references lean fat/groovy
   display faces that are off-brand; #4 is the closest bridge — bold condensed sans paired
   with italic serif.
5. **Real vocabulary over praise words** (§9). *Abol · Tona · Baraka*, jebena, buna, Yirgacheffe.

## Brief for image generation

Paste this, then attach the reference images.

> I need original artwork for a coffee shop's t-shirt line. The brand is Hasna's, an Ethiopian
> coffee house in Austin, Texas.
>
> Style: single-color flat vector print, no gradients, no shading, no photographic elements.
> 1930s rubber-hose cartoon linework for any character work — think vintage mascot, clean
> confident outlines, slightly irregular and hand-drawn rather than mechanically perfect.
>
> Palette: artwork is cream (#F8F4EE) on a dark espresso (#1A0C05) garment. One ink color only.
> Accent colors exist but appear ONLY as thin parallel rules, never as fills: green (#4A7C59),
> gold (#B8860B), oxblood (#9E2B25).
>
> Subject vocabulary — use the real objects, not generic coffee iconography:
> - jebena — the Ethiopian clay brewing pot, round body, long narrow neck, small spout, domed lid
> - sini — small handleless cups
> - rekebot — the wooden stand the cups sit on
> - coffee branches with leaves and cherries
> - geometric tilet border motifs — diamonds, chevrons, stepped crosses
>
> No generic espresso machines, no paper to-go cups, no Italian moka pots. The specificity is
> the point.
>
> Composition: large back print. Centered, roughly square, designed to sit well on an oversized
> boxy tee. Leave the type to me — generate the illustration on a transparent background.
